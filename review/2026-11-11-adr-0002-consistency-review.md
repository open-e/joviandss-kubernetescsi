## Verdict

The record is consistent in its structure and in almost all of its numbers: anchors, Table of Contents, List of Tables, risk, step, table and priority numbering, 163 code references, the cited files, the typespec and OpenAPI figures, the hurl Finding numbers and every count from 34 path strings to 21,000 requests per run all hold. What remains is one misstatement of the decision's history, raised by the user during this review, one factual contradiction in the Decision, six places where the body is ahead of or behind its own open questions and catalogues, and a tail of small leftovers from the merge. None of them needs new evidence; five need a decision from the user.

## High

1. **The Options section weighs alternatives that were never on the table.** The user, 2026-11-11: there were no considerations regarding v3; it will not be supported; there are no alternatives; v4 is the one we move on with. The record says otherwise: the intro (L350–354) announces "two choices weighed in full", Option A2 "keep v3" (L373–380) and Option A3 "both versions, chosen by a setting or detected at start" (L382–390) are written up with advantages and disadvantages, the Decision says "a reader who puts compatibility with older JovianDSS 1 releases first would choose Option A3" (L486–487) and "Decided by the user: Option A1 on 2026-11-05" (L489), and Advice says the first version "rejected" keep-v3 and both-versions (L1663–1665). What the change would be, following the convention's source (ceejbot: "If you only considered one option, say so and explain why"): the version stays in Options considered as group A, but as one subsection, "Option A: v4 only, the only option considered", which says so and explains why (v3 is deprecated and ends with JovianDSS 1, JovianDSS 2 serves only v4, the Proxmox plugin already uses v4, the move changes no behaviour on JovianDSS 1: key observations 1 and 2) and names the price the one option carries (an older JovianDSS 1 release without v4 stops working, risk 2; the minimum release has to be stated and kept true, Stage 1). A2 and A3 go, with the intro's "two choices weighed in full" (L350–354) and the Decision's "would choose Option A3" (L486–487); "Decided by the user: Option A1" (L489) becomes "v4 only"; Advice (L1663–1665) says the first version listed keep-v3 and both-versions and the user ruled them out as never real. B1 to B5 stay as they are, with their anchors; the links to A1 (ToC, L487, L489) follow the new heading in the same change. The README now carries the rule, and "with the version choice" in it was corrected today.
2. **The Decision counts question 25 as taken.** L497–499: "every ID and name is validated at the CSI boundary ... (questions 20, 21, 25)". Question 25 is open (L1446, L1458), and the validation item itself cites it as open (L874–876). Either drop 25 from that list or decide 25.
